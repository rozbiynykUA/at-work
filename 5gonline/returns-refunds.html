Returns & Refunds

We want you to love your purchase. If not, we’ll make it right. Learn how to return a product or request a refund below.

Policy Overview

  • Processing Time

    Refunds and returns are processed within 2–3 business days after we receive your request.

  • Return Window

    Products can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

  • Refund Method

    Refunds are issued to your original payment method.

    Depending on your bank, it may take 2–5 business days for the refund to appear.
