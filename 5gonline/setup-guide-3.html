Quick and simple installation instructions for your 4G and 5G routers. Choose your model below to get started.

Setup Guide for

Huawei 5G CPE

  • SIM Card

    Insert your nano-SIM card while the router is off.

  • Plug In

    Connect the power adapter and wait for the blue LED.

  • Connect

    Connect the power adapter and wait for the blue LED.

  • Login

    Go to 192.168.8.1, enter admin credentials.

  • Update

    Finish setup wizard & check firmware.

Having Issues?

  • LED stays red → Check SIM alignment
  • No Internet → Restart your router
  • Slow speed → Place near window
