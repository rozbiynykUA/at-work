Shipping & Delivery

Fast, Secure, and Reliable Delivery

Delivery Options

  • Standard Delivery

    1–3 business days across the Netherlands.

  • Express Shipping

    Same-day or next-day delivery in select regions.

  • International Shipping

    Available for EU countries. Estimated 3–7 business days.

Delivery Times & Order Tracking

  • Orders placed before 15:00 CET are shipped the same day.
  • Orders placed after 15:00 ship the next business day.
  • You’ll receive a tracking number via email once your order leaves our warehouse.
  • Tracking is available within 12 hours after dispatch.

Shipping Partners

We work with trusted carriers to ensure fast and reliable delivery across the Netherlands and Europe.

Shipping Guarantee & Policy Highlights

  • Free Shipping

    For orders over €99

  • Easy Returns

    Return within 30 days

  • Secure Packaging

    All items are safety-sealed

  • Warranty Included

    All products include a 2-year warranty
